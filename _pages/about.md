I am a full-stack software engineer and solution architect with 20+ years of experience turning complex ideas into practical, maintainable systems. This site is a showcase of the projects, technical decisions, and lessons behind my work across web, cloud, mobile, AI, blockchain, data, and embedded development.

I enjoy working at the point where product needs meet hard engineering problems: designing reliable services, improving performance, integrating emerging technologies, and shipping useful tools end to end.

## What I Build

- **Distributed and cloud systems** - event-driven services, APIs, microservices, data pipelines, observability, and production operations.
- **High-performance applications** - practical use of Go, Rust, TypeScript, C#, Python, and C++ where each technology best fits the problem.
- **AI-enabled automation** - systems that apply AI to reduce repetitive work, surface useful decisions, and improve developer productivity.
- **Blockchain and DeFi products** - Solana integrations, wallet and transaction flows, DEX and market-data systems, and performance-sensitive trading infrastructure.
- **Cross-platform experiences** - modern web applications, mobile apps, games, data visualizations, and connected embedded devices.

## How I Work

I bring a systems mindset to every project: clarify the problem, choose technology deliberately, make trade-offs visible, and build for the people who will run and maintain the result. My work spans discovery through delivery, including architecture, implementation, integration, testing, monitoring, and iteration.

The Solana trading-system series remains an important case study here: it documents how I approach polyglot architecture, low-latency data paths, risk-aware automation, and observability. Alongside it, I write about the broader tools and techniques that help me build better software.
