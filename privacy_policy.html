Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications (the "Apps") published by Guidebee (James Shen) ("we", "us", or "our") on Google Play and other app stores. It explains what information the Apps may collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or using the Apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We designed our Apps to collect as little information as possible. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address unless you voluntarily provide it to us (for example, by contacting us for support).

Depending on the App, the following information may be processed:

  • Information you provide: content you enter into an App is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us unless the App clearly states otherwise.
  • Device and usage information: non-personal technical data such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash logs, and general usage statistics, which help us fix bugs and improve the Apps.
  • Advertising identifiers: if an App displays ads, the advertising provider may use your device's advertising ID to show ads and measure their performance.
  • Location: some Apps (for example, map or navigation apps) may request access to your device location to provide their core features. Location data is used on your device for that purpose only and is not sold or shared with us, except as processed by the third-party services listed below.

2. Device Permissions

Apps may request Android permissions (such as internet access, location, camera, or storage) only when needed for a specific feature. You can grant or revoke permissions at any time in your device's settings. Declining a permission may limit certain features but will not otherwise affect your use of the App.

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Apps' features.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • To understand how the Apps are used so we can improve them.
  • To display advertising in ad-supported Apps.

We do not sell your personal information.
